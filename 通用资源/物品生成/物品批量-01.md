## 生成提示词

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one production game-icon contact sheet for the ZooV7 cozy zoo game. Generate ONE 1024x1024 image containing exactly 16 separate icons in a precise invisible 4-column by 4-row grid of equal 256x256 cells.

STYLE LOCK: Same pixel craft as a bright cozy zoo map: lawn green, sand-path cream, pond blue, sunflower yellow, and pale oak. Chubby rounded objects, visible medium pixel grain, crisp stepped edges, clean color blocks, thin dark-olive outlines. High-key and saturated, not washed out. Not glossy plastic, not thick chocolate frames, not 8-bit, not gray, not photoreal. Flat front view, zero perspective, orthographic, every object face-on. Even soft light. No cast shadows, no glossy highlights. All 16 icons MUST share the same pixel scale, outline thickness, rendering detail, palette family and light direction. Clear recognizable silhouettes at 64x64 display size. No blurry antialiasing or painterly texture.

LAYOUT LOCK: One isolated centered icon per cell, at x=128,384,640,896 and y=128,384,640,896. Each object must fit entirely within its own cell, with at least 32px empty margin on all sides; roughly 160-184px longest dimension. No overlaps. No visible grid, borders, icon plates, circles behind objects, captions, labels, numbers, watermark, scenery or extra decorations. Transparent background if supported; otherwise perfectly uniform pure white background without any shadows, gradient or checkerboard.

EXACT CONTENT, left-to-right, top-to-bottom:
Row 1: one face-on round gold coin without lettering; one blue faceted diamond gem; one yellow lightning bolt; one warm red heart.
Row 2: one yellow five-point star; one closed pale-oak treasure chest with gold fittings; one wrapped gift box with a ribbon; one gold key.
Row 3: one cream zoo admission ticket with notched sides and no text; one simple round clock with two clearly distinct hands and no numerals; one gold trophy cup with two handles; one gold crown.
Row 4: one small green-and-tan backpack; one short pale-oak log with visible cut-end growth rings; one fresh green leaf; one simple red meat cut with a small cream bone.

The chest, gift box and backpack must be clearly different silhouettes. Do not replace any requested object with another icon. Exactly sixteen icons, no extras.
