[STYLE]
Same pixel craft as a bright cozy zoo map: lawn green, sand-path cream, pond blue, sunflower yellow, and the pale oak of wooden animal signs. Chubby rounded ends, visible medium pixel grain, crisp edges, clean color blocks, thin dark-olive outlines. High-key and saturated. Not glossy, not 8-bit, not photoreal.

[CAMERA]
flat front view, zero perspective, orthographic, face-on.

[LIGHTING]
even soft light. One-pixel darker rim. No cast shadows, no glossy highlights.

[ASSETS]
Two separate widgets, stacked, with a wide empty gap between them. They must not touch or nest.
- xp track 720x80, the background bar: a chunky horizontal capsule, pale oak rim, flat cream groove inside, empty, no fill, no icon, no text. Left cap and right cap are matching rounded ends. The long middle is a flat repeatable band so it can be 9-sliced horizontally.
- xp fill 720x52, the foreground bar: a shorter solid lawn-green capsule, same rounded caps, flat green middle for a horizontal 9-slice. No track behind it, no icon, no text.

[BACKGROUND]
Fully transparent. Real alpha channel, every empty pixel alpha 0.
No cream backdrop, no white, no gray, no checkerboard, no floor, no shadow plate.
Cream exists only inside the track groove. The gap around both bars is empty transparency.

[RESTRICTIONS]
no words, no digits, no labels, no watermark, no animals, no people. No overlapping. These bars are tall capsules, not hairlines. The canvas background must be transparent, not a solid color.
